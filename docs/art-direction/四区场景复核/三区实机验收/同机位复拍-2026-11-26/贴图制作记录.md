# 贴图制作记录

公园日间草地使用一张无投影的可平铺底色图。着色器在真实地面上组合近处草梢、疏密变化和大片土色，明暗取决于游戏日照。裁缝铺使用窄幅画布作为两扇橱窗后墙的陈列纹理，真实窗框、雨棚、布架和营业状态在它前面。以下是两张新贴图的原始生成描述。

## 公园草地

Square seamless repeating Unity albedo texture for a hand-painted contemporary Chinese neighborhood park in an isometric illustrated life sim. Orthographic view straight down of just a 6 by 6 metre area of short tended grass, opaque edge-to-edge. Soft olive and fresh spring greens on warm earth, gouache and colored pencil, broken strokes, blade clusters and very occasional clover as tiny tapered brush marks. About two-thirds calm broad pigment fields, one-third fine detail concentrated in small irregular islands. Carefully avoid any directional light, cast shadows, perspective, horizon, vignette, isolated big plants, borders, text, streets and props. Mid-values around sRGB 113,138,66 for the grassy field, ochre green highlights and a few cooler earthy accents. High quality painted diffuse texture, visually even across four edges, completely tileable and clean.

## 裁缝店橱窗

Wide horizontal panoramic hand-painted albedo texture for the interior back wall of a small tailor's shop in a pleasant Chinese neighborhood, seen straight-on from the street at eye height. One continuous two-bay sewing atelier, warm honey wood, amber diffuse bounced light, dusty navy, celadon green, cream, muted terracotta bolts of fabric. Left half has a gentle diagonal rhythm of upright short cloth bolts, low stacks of neatly folded fabric under a wooden counter and a little old black sewing machine silhouette. Right half has a dress form wearing an unfinished cream blouse, brass shears on a work table and shelves with spools. Focused simple readable groups, living detail at the scale of a miniature game viewed from above. Refined semi-realistic gouache on paper with delicate charcoal edges and visible fine drybrush, shallow believable interior depth. Edge-to-edge interior, no shop exterior, no window frame or door drawn on top, no people, no house fronts, no lettering, no logos, absolutely no glass reflections or strong cast shadows. Middle values rich and legible, warm not orange. Approximately 3:1 horizontal composition meant to be cropped into a left and a right window inset. Primary purpose painterly material on real 3d shelf recesses.

## 住宅和行道树

城郊保留两种现有住宅的模型和立面。砖面、抹灰、卷材屋面、陶瓦、人行道和日照分开校准。现代街区的行道树采用实体树干、分叉和分散的带透明轮廓的叶簇。叶簇从已确认的社区概念中取原生叶扇与较大枝团，完整轮廓留出透明边界，再装入树木图集。它们会在游戏时间和光照下投影。
